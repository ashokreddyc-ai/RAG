Payment Gateway Documentation
==============================

1. Payment Failure
A payment may fail when the issuing bank declines the card, the account has insufficient funds, or the card has expired.
If a payment fails, check the transaction status before asking the customer to retry.
Customers can try another payment method if the issuing bank declines the card.

2. Transaction Timeout
A transaction timeout occurs when the payment gateway does not receive a response within the expected time.
A timeout does not always mean that the payment failed.
Before retrying, verify the transaction status to avoid duplicate payments.

3. Refund Processing
Refund processing time depends on the payment method and the issuing bank.
After a refund is initiated, save the refund reference number for tracking.
If the refund has not arrived within the expected processing period, contact the payment support team.

4. Fraud Prevention
Review unusual transaction amounts, repeated payment attempts, and mismatches between billing and device locations.
Do not approve a transaction based only on a single risk signal.
Follow the organization's fraud review process and document the reason for any manual decision.

5. Support Escalation
When escalating an issue, include the transaction ID, timestamp, payment method, error message, and troubleshooting steps already completed.
Never include full card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive authentication information in a support ticket.

--------------------
Transaction ID: TXN-2026-00125
Issue: Customer reports that the payment status is pending.

Next action: If the status remains unclear, escalate to the payment operations team with the transaction ID and timestamp.
